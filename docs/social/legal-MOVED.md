# The legal documents moved — 2026-09-20

They are now at **`docs/legal/`**, because they cover every NexLink Product
rather than NexLink Social alone, and because they are published in English and
German.

- Sources: `docs/legal/en/`, `docs/legal/de/`
- Shared definitions appendix: `docs/legal/definitions.{en,de}.md`
- **What ships:** `docs/legal/dist/`, assembled by `tools/legal/build-legal.py`
- The Contact Page every document cites: `web/contact/index.html`

The copies that used to live here were deleted rather than left behind. Two
copies of a legal document is how they come to disagree, and the whole point of
the new layout is that a defined term cannot mean two things.
