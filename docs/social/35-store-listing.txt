FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 char limit) — about 2,050 characters
------------------------------------------------------------
NexLink Social is a small, private messenger.

Your messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted. They are scrambled on your
phone and can only be unscrambled by the people you are talking to — not by us,
and not by anyone who gets hold of the server.

It is invite only. You cannot sign up without a code from someone already here,
and there is no directory to browse. Nobody can find you by your phone number or
your email address, because we never ask for either.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Send messages, photos and files
• Make voice and video calls
• React, reply, edit and delete
• Search your own messages, on your own phone
• Use more than one device, and verify each one so you know it is really yours
• Move your history to a new phone with a recovery key

WHAT WE CAN AND CANNOT SEE
Most apps are vague about this. We would rather not be.

We cannot see the content of your messages, the content of your calls, your
photos and files, or your message history.

We can see who you message and when, roughly how often and how much, your IP
address, and which devices you use. Google's push service is told that a message
arrived for you — never what it says.

The app shows you this, in exactly these words, before you create an account.

NO ACCOUNT ATTACHED TO YOU
An account here is a username and a password. No phone number, no email address,
no real name. There are no ads, and there is no analytics or tracking code in the
app.

HONEST LIMITS
This is run by one person, not a company. There is no support team and no uptime
guarantee, and it is offered as-is.

It is early software. It works, and it will have rough edges.

If you lose both your recovery key and your phone, your message history is gone.
That is what end-to-end encryption actually means, and nobody — including us —
can undo it for you. The app will ask you to set up a recovery key. Do it.

You need an invite code to create an account. If you do not have one, this app
will not be much use to you yet.

From the maker of NexLink.
