Delete your NexLink Social account

This removes your account from the server. It cannot be undone.

You do not need the app installed to do this.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, and every device signed in to it
  • Your display name and picture
  • Photos, videos and files you uploaded
  • Your encrypted message backup
  • Your email address, if you gave one
  • The record of which addresses you connected from

What is kept, and why

  • Your username — kept permanently so nobody can register it later and be mistaken for you.
  • That you accepted the terms, and confirmed your age — kept as the record that you did.
  • The invite you joined with — a one-way code and timestamps, kept so abuse can be traced.

Your messages are end-to-end encrypted, so the server has never been able to read them and has nothing of yours to hand over or keep.
