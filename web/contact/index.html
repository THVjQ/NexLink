What to use this address for

  • Privacy requests — access, correction, objection, restriction, or anything the app does not expose. Expect a reply within 30 days.
  • Formal notices under the Terms of Service.
  • Security reports — see the Security and Vulnerability Disclosure Policy first. There is no bug bounty.
  • Law enforcement requests — read the Law Enforcement Guidelines first. Message content does not exist in readable form and cannot be produced.
  • Appeals against a moderation decision.

What this address is not

  • Not an emergency channel. One person reads this, and they may be asleep. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number.
  • Not a support desk. There is no support commitment and no guaranteed response time.
  • Not a route to your messages. They are end-to-end encrypted. No request to this address can recover a lost message history, and no password reset can either.
